Our story

Forged by nature. Grounded in tradition.

Mejillas begins in the highlands of Mexico, where the land is carved by sun, wind, and history — and where agave withstands scorching heat and rocky terrain for years, only to rise stronger and sweeter with time.

The brand is a partnership: the innovators behind Melo Beverage NA Company, and one of the region's most respected multi-generation agave-growing families — farmers who understand not just how to grow agave, but how to honor it. Every bottle starts as hand-selected mature agave, slow-roasted the traditional way, naturally fermented, and distilled with precision in small batches.

The name? Mejillas means cheeks — a testament to strength, endurance, and quiet power, qualities the agave shares with the people who raise it.
