Our story

Forged by nature. Grounded in tradition.

Mejillas begins in Los Altos de Jalisco, where iron-red soil and high-altitude sun push blue agave to grow slowly and concentrate its sugars.

Our agave comes from a single family of growers — the same fields, the same hands, for three generations. We harvest each piña only when the jimador says it is ready, never by calendar. Then we roast slow, ferment with patience, and distill in small batches at our partner distillery in Jalisco.

The name? Mejillas means cheeks — a nod to the warm, flushed faces around a table where the tequila is good and nobody is in a hurry.
