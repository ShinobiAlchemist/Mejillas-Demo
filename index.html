Our story

Forged by nature. Grounded in tradition.

In the highlands of Mexico, agave withstands scorching heat, rocky terrain, and years of waiting — and rises stronger and sweeter for it. Mejillas pairs the innovators behind Melo Beverage with a multi-generation family of agave growers: hand-selected piñas, slow-roasted, naturally fermented, and bottled only when we would pour it at our own table.
