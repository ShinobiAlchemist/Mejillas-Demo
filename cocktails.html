Cocktails & pairings

Six recipes worth the good bottle

Every recipe names its expression, and every pairing has been argued over at our own table. Measures are metric; conviction is optional.

Blanco

Rose Gold Paloma

Ingredients

  • 60 ml Mejillas Blanco
  • Grapefruit soda, to top
  • 15 ml fresh lime juice
  • Pinch of sea salt

Build over ice in a tall glass, squeeze in the lime, top with soda, and stir once.

Pairing: fish tacos or anything off the grill with citrus.

Blanco

Highlands Margarita

Ingredients

  • 50 ml Mejillas Blanco
  • 25 ml fresh lime juice
  • 15 ml orange liqueur
  • 10 ml agave nectar

Shake hard with ice and strain over fresh rocks. Salt rim optional, lime wheel not.

Pairing: ceviche, oysters, or good salty chips.

Reposado

Reposado Honey Sour

Ingredients

  • 50 ml Mejillas Reposado
  • 25 ml fresh lemon juice
  • 20 ml honey syrup (2:1)
  • Optional egg white

Shake (dry shake first if using egg white), strain into a coupe, garnish with a lemon twist.

Pairing: aged cheeses or roast chicken.

Extra Añejo

Cheeks Old Fashioned

Ingredients

  • 60 ml Mejillas Extra Añejo
  • 5 ml agave syrup
  • 2 dashes mole bitters
  • Wide orange peel

Stir with ice until properly cold, strain over one large cube, express the orange peel.

Pairing: dark chocolate, churros, or a quiet evening.

Mezcal

Smoke & Ember Negroni

Ingredients

  • 30 ml Mejillas Mezcal
  • 30 ml Campari
  • 30 ml sweet vermouth
  • Orange peel

Stir with ice, strain over a big cube, garnish with the peel. Equal parts, unequal results.

Pairing: grilled meats or blue cheese.

Any expression

Neat, with Sangrita

Ingredients

  • A pour of your Mejillas of choice
  • Tomato and orange juice
  • Fresh lime, a little chile

Serve the sangrita alongside — sip, don't shoot. Alternate between the two and take your time.

Pairing: the table itself. This is how the highlands drink.
