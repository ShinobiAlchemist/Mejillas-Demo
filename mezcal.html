Tasting notes

Nose. Smoked agave and wild herbs.

Palate. Earthy and mineral-rich, with a gentle touch of sweet smoke.

Finish. Bold, lingering, and artisanal.

How to pour it

Neat with orange slices and sal de gusano (the highland way), or stirred into the Smoke & Ember Negroni.
